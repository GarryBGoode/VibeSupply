**Chosen: fsw = 300 kHz, hs = ls = CSD18531Q5A** (TI 60 V NexFET, SON 5×6, specified at VGS = 4.5 V).
- Lowest loss at every hard point. The bottom FET's Qoss and Qrr are dissipated in the *top* FET, so a low-charge bottom FET
  (CSD18531Q5A: Qoss 32 nC, Qrr 100 nC @300 A/µs) beats a low-Rds(on) one (CSD18540Q5B: 83 nC / 145 nC). One part number for both.
- LCSC stock is thin (TI original C2876524: 35 pcs); the pin-compatible-class CSD18532Q5B (302 pcs) costs ~0.2 W more.
- 300 kHz, not 200 kHz: 200 kHz would save ~0.8 W, but in LTspice the LTC7803 then shows period doubling (sub-harmonic
  oscillation) above ~80 % duty. Its internal slope-compensation ramp is a fixed voltage per cycle, so a lower frequency
  means a slower ramp against the same inductor down-slope (sim/results.md).
- 5 V gate drive (LTC7803 TG pull-up 3 Ω) makes the transitions slower than with the LTC7801's 10 V drive; Qrr and V·I switching
  are the biggest top-FET terms.

## 3. Thermal — fanless, the aluminium extrusion is the heatsink

Model: all heat ends up in the extrusion, which sheds it to the room: Rθ(enclosure→ambient) = 1/(h·A) = 1/(9 W/m²K × 0.0290 m²) ≈ **3.8 K/W** (extrusion 120 × 78 × 43 mm outside, only the 4 long faces — the end caps and the front add-on are printed; anodized or painted assumed, bare aluminium radiates poorly → ~1.5× worse).
Heat capacity ≈ 190 J/K (155 g aluminium + board) → thermal time constant ≈ **12 min**.
FET junction → extrusion ≈ 1 (jc) + 4 (via array) + 1.5 (gap pad) = **6.5 K/W** (ASSUMPTION; board in the lowest slot, 7.06 mm above the floor → ~5.5 mm Al spacer + gap pad). Ambient 30 °C.

| Case | Heat W | Extrusion °C (steady) | Top FET Tj °C | Time to 55 °C from cold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Worst continuous buck load, no regen | 8.0 | 61 | 80 | 21 min |
| Typical heavy use (half the worst loss) | 4.0 | 45 | 65 | never |
| Worst buck load + clamp at 10 W average | 18.0 | 99 | 118 | 5 min |
| Clamp 10 W average only (regen, buck idle) | 12.0 | 76 | 95 | 10 min |

- Continuous heat budget for a ≤ 55 °C enclosure: **6.5 W** in total (housekeeping 2 W included). The worst buck point (8.0 W) is above it: full load runs for the time in the table, then the firmware throttles current from the enclosure NTC. Typical heavy use never gets there.
- Worst FET losses: top 3.01 W, bottom 0.89 W → only ~20 K above the extrusion. The FETs are not the problem; **the enclosure surface temperature is**.
- Regen clamp: its average must come out of the same budget. With the buck idle ≈ 4.5 W continuous; firmware derates it from the enclosure NTC (e.g. ~5 W below 45 °C, tapering to ~1 W at 55 °C). Bursts are fine: 50 W / 10 s = 500 J raises the extrusion ≈ 3 K.
- Parts on the extrusion: both buck FETs and the clamp FET (bottom-side via arrays → gap pad → spacer → floor), the LTO100 clamp resistor (screwed to the wall, wired to the board). The inductor (~0.7 W) and shunts stay on the board.
- 3D-printed add-on and end caps sit against a ≤ 60 °C extrusion: PETG or ASA, not PLA (softens around 60 °C).

## 4. Inductor and controller current sense

- Inductor: Coilcraft SER2918H-103KL, 10 µH, Isat 32.1 A, DCR ≤ 2.86 mΩ (hot ≈ 3.72 mΩ) → 0.37 W copper at 10 A. Same body and footprint as the SER2918H-682 of the 20 A design.
  Alternatives on LCSC: XAL1510-682MED (6.8 µH, 4.6 mΩ, Isat 36 A, 15×16 mm, C3911560); HCZC-2918HT-6R8-M (2.45 mΩ, 45 A, C53202464).
- Worst ripple 2.6 A pp (26 % of 10 A; LTC recommends ~30–40 %) → peak at 10 A output 11.3 A.
- R_SENSE = 2.0 mΩ (2× 4 mΩ 2512) → cycle-by-cycle peak limit at low duty 22.5 / 25.0 / 27.5 A (min/typ/max).
  Min limit leaves 11.2 A above the 10 A peak (the MCU-set ITH clamp is the working limit, this is the backstop); max limit (27.5 A) is below the inductor Isat (32.1 A).
- R_SENSE dissipation at 10 A: 0.20 W.
- **The limit shrinks at high duty** (slope compensation): in LTspice, with ITH at its maximum, 2.5 mΩ delivered only ~9.4 A at
  D ≈ 0.9 (27 V from 30 V); 2.0 mΩ holds 27 V to ~9.4 A and gives 10 A down to ~26.5 V; 1.5 mΩ holds 27 V past 11.5 A but its
  low-duty limit (up to 37 A) would exceed the inductor Isat. → 2.0 mΩ: **10 A is available up to ≈ Vin − 3.5 V** (26.5 V from 30 V).
  From USB-C this never matters: above ~13 V the 140 W budget limits the current first.
- Sense-signal ripple ΔIL·R_SENSE ≈ 5.2 mV (LTC suggests 10–20 mV for noise immunity): below that, so the sense
  traces need a clean Kelvin layout and the datasheet's RC filter at the SENSE pins.
- Min on-time 40 ns → min duty 1.2 %: below 0.37 V out (31 V in) / 0.34 V (28 V in) / 0.11 V (9 V PD in) the controller pulse-skips (higher ripple, still regulates).
- Path drops alone would allow ~27.7 V at 10 A from 28 V, but the LTC7803 does not reach 100 % duty under load: slope
  compensation caps the current near dropout. LTspice: 30 V → 10 A up to 26.5 V; 28 V PD → ≈ 26.5 V at ≈ 4.3 A (sim/results.md t5, t7).

  100 V MLCCs instead of 50 V: at 30 V a 4.7 µF/100 V part keeps about as much capacitance as a 10 µF/50 V one, with more voltage margin.
- Input ripple (ceramics only, 10 A, D=0.5): ≈ 0.46 V pp.
- Damping vs. ~1 µH of source cable: Z0 = √(L/C) ≈ 0.24 Ω → bulk cap ESR in that range damps the input resonance.
- PD rule: keep ≤ 10 µF on raw VBUS *before* the sink switch; the bulk sits after the LM74800.
- LTC7803 VIN pin: RC filter (≈ 10 Ω + 1 µF) + ~36 V zener/TVS at the pin (40 V abs max); see calc/ic_reshop.md.

## 6. Output capacitors and post-filter

- C1 (after the main L): 4× 4.7 µF 100 V 1210 (≈12 µF eff. at 27 V) + damper 100 µF polymer with 0.1 Ω in series.
- Post filter: 0.47 µH FXL1040-R47 (kept; 30 A, 1.7 mΩ) → C2: 4× 4.7 µF 100 V 1210 + 100 µF 63 V polymer (24 mΩ).
- Ripple (fundamental, worst ΔIL 2.6 A pp): at C1 ≈ 105 mV pp, at the output ≈ **2.3 mV pp** (target ≤ 20 mV).
- Post-filter resonance ≈ 21.9 kHz (with the polymer cap); CV-loop crossover must stay well below → see §9.
- Energy in the output caps at 27 V ≈ 82 mJ (dumped into a short before CC takes over).

## 7. Sensing and setpoint scaling

- CV divider 1/10.2 (e.g. 91.9 k / 10 k, 0.1 %, 25 ppm): DAC full scale 2.9 V → 29.6 V out; 27 V → 2.65 V. 12-bit DAC → 7.2 mV per LSB at the output.
- CC: INA240A3 (gain 100, LCSC C2060584) across the 2.0 mΩ inductor shunt → 0.200 V/A; DAC full scale 2.9 V → 14.5 A, 3.5 mA per LSB.
  INA240 offset ±25 µV max → ±12 mA error; firmware trims the setpoint from the INA228 reading.
- Metering: INA228 on the 1 mΩ output shunt; ±40.96 mV range (ADCRANGE=1) → up to 40 A, LSB ≈ 24 µA with CURRENT_LSB = 12.5 A/2^19; bus voltage 195 µV/LSB.
- Shunt dissipation at 10 A: output 0.10 W, input 0.10 W.
- Option for later: external dual 16-bit DAC (e.g. DAC80502) → ~0.4 mV / 0.2 mA resolution. Leave a footprint.

## 9. Loop sanity check (frequency domain)

(Q = 0.64, i.e. adequate slope compensation), feeding C1 (+ damper) → post-filter L → C2 ∥ polymer ∥ load.
